📖 How to Play Baby Watch

🎯 Objective

Guide baby crawling through 3 lanes at home. Collect healthy food to keep energy high. Avoid junk food — eating 3 pieces of junk costs a life. Run as far as you can!

🎮 Controls

  • Tap left side of screen → move baby one lane left
  • Tap right side → move baby one lane right
  • Swipe left/right → also changes lane
  • Arrow keys work on desktop

FoodEffect

🍭 Candy−20 energy + lose life

🍼 Elixir Lives

  • You start with 3 Elixir lives (shown as bottles in HUD)
  • Each junk food eaten = −1 life
  • Lose all 3 → Baby gets sick! Game over
  • Reach 2000m → Bonus life restored!

⚡ Energy Bar

  • Energy drains slowly as baby crawls
  • Healthy food restores energy
  • Energy hits zero → Baby exhausted! Game over
